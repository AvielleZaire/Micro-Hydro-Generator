THE PRODUCT

HOW IT WORKS

A micro hydrogenerator works by converting the kinetic energy into electrical energy.The flowing water will come from the tank and will go down directly to the turbine in which the alternator is connected. The trurbine then converts the kinetic energy to mechanical energy while the alternator converts the mechanical energy to electrical energy.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Turbine

A turbine is a device that converts the kinetic energy of a fluid or gas into mechanical energy. In the context of power generation, a turbine is typically driven by steam or water and is used to rotate an alternator.

Alternator

An alternator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy. It works by using a rotating magnetic field to induce an electrical current in a conductor. Alernators are used in modern power generation system.

Inverter

An inverter is a device that converts DC power to AC power. This is necessary because the electrical power produced by the alternator is typically in the form of AC power, but many modern devices require DC power.

Battery

Battery energy storage is a rapidly growing technology. The batteries can capture excess energy produced by the hydro turbines, which would have been otherwise spilled due to low demand or excess waterflow.
